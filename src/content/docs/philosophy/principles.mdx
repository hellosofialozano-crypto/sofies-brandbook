# Principles

## 01. Intentionality

Every decision has a reason.

A component exists because it solves a problem.

A pattern exists because it creates consistency.

---

## 02. Precision

Quality comes from details.

Spacing.
Typography.
Architecture.
Documentation.

Small decisions create exceptional experiences.

---

## 03. Restraint

Good design knows what to remove.

Complexity should never exist without purpose.

---

## 04. Systems Thinking

Individual decisions become ecosystems.

Every project contributes to a larger language.

---

## 05. Continuous Editing

Software is never static.

It evolves through observation, refinement, and iteration.
