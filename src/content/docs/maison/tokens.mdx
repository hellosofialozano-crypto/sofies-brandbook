# Design Tokens

Tokens are the smallest decisions inside Maison.

They transform design choices into reusable values.

---

## Token Philosophy

A value should never exist without meaning.

Avoid:#FFFFFF


Prefer:

surface.primary


---

### Semantic

Meaning-based decisions.

Example: color.neutral.900


---

### Semantic

Meaning-based decisions.

Example:

text.primary


---

### Component

Specific usage.

Example:

button.background.primary


---

## Principle

Tokens allow SOFI'ES products to evolve without breaking consistency.
