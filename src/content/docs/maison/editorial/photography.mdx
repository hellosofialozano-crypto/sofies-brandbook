# Photography

Images inside SOFI'ES should communicate atmosphere.

---

## Characteristics

Preferred:

- editorial photography
- natural lighting
- tactile materials
- human moments
- artistic composition

---

## Avoid

- generic stock imagery
- excessive effects
- artificial technology imagery

---

## Purpose

Photography creates emotion.

The interface creates interaction.

Together they create experience.
