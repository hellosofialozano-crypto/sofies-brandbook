# Editorial Direction

Maison approaches software like an editorial object.

Every element has:

- intention
- placement
- rhythm
- purpose

---

## The Maison Feeling

The experience should feel:

- considered
- elegant
- calm
- precise

---

## Visual Principles

### Contrast

Technology and humanity.

Precision and emotion.

Structure and expression.

---

### Space

Empty space is an active design element.

It creates:

- focus
- hierarchy
- importance

---

### Curation

Not everything deserves attention.

Maison prioritizes what matters.
